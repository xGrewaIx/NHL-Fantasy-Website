# Project Overview

## What is the problem?

Fantasy hockey managers often have pre season rankings which help them determine who to draft. In my experience as the season goes on most tend to continue to use those pre season rankings to inform their decision regarding their players in trades, waiver, etc. Most do not have access to a model that takes the current seasons player performance, linesmates, shooting %, etc, into account. 

The goal is the build a platform that solves that issue. To have a live fantasy ranking that updates everyday once all the scheduled games are played. Fantasy managers will have access to a rankings board which provides rankings based on the current day, not the pre season rankings. 

Overall, the platform should combine hockey analytics with configurable fantasy scoring to provide rankings that update automatically as new NHL games are played.
